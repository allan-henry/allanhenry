I photograph sports for the USA TODAY Sports and Imagn Images wire, based in Phoenix. I have been on contract since 2009, first for US Presswire, then Gannett, then USA TODAY Sports, now Imagn. That work includes three seasons assigned full time to the PGA TOUR, more than 700 credentialed events, and more than 25,000 published frames carrying my byline, with covers and double trucks along the way and Photo of the Year honors from Sports Illustrated, Golf Magazine, and Golf Digest.

The frames on this site are the ones I would put in front of an editor. Everywhere my credit has run, as it ran, is archived at the tearsheets site below.
